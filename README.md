# Jodi Intelligence

Jodi Intelligence is an evidence-grounded life-planning intelligence layer for matrimonial platforms and professional matchmakers.

It is designed for couples who have mutually expressed interest and want help discussing practical expectations before commitment.

> This product is a conversation aid. It is not a compatibility score, marriage recommendation, or marriage-success predictor.

## Level 1 MVP

The Level 1 engine compares structured answers across five life-planning dimensions:

1. Career and location
2. Children timeline
3. Family care
4. Money decisions
5. Household responsibilities

It produces:

- alignments
- expectation gaps
- importance-aware discussion priorities
- evidence-backed guided scenarios
- validated AI wording for important gaps
